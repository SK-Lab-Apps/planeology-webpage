| Stress concentration explanation: a very localized area of much higher stress; with rounded modern windows the stress flows around the curved edges with minimal build-up; with the Comet's squarish windows it could not flow around the abrupt corners; the prototype's passenger windows were squarish | FAA Lessons Learned Comet page | ✅ Paraphrased |
